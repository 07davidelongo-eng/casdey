casdey · brand guide · v1, august 2026

casdey is deliberately generic, not tied to any one product, so the identity can outlast a pivot without touching the domain, the email, or the name. The mark stays quiet on purpose: lowercase, unadorned, built to sit comfortably next to whatever the product becomes next.

wordmark: Raleway Light text: Inter data: JetBrains Mono

01 · Wordmark

Lowercase, always.
Even mid-sentence.

"casdey" is never capitalized, not at the start of a sentence, not in a headline, not in a signature. The lowercase is the whole personality: quiet, unforced, a little understated. Treat it as a fixed spelling, not a style choice to be turned on and off.

On paper — #F6F4F1

On white — #FFFFFF

Reversed — #0D0D0D

Clearspace

Keep clear space equal to the height of the lowercase "c" on every side. Nothing else sits inside that margin.

Don't

  • Capitalize any letter, ever
  • Recolor the mark outside Ink, Paper, or White
  • Add a drop shadow, outline, or gradient
  • Set a tagline directly beneath it with no clearspace
  • Stretch, condense, or re-kern the letterforms

02 · Color

Grayscale first.
One quiet accent.

The palette is built from the wordmark itself, an ink pulled straight from the logo file and a paper it already sits on, then a ramp of warm neutrals between them. A single accent exists only to mark what's interactive. It never fills a page.

Paper #F6F4F1 Default background
White #FFFFFF Raised surfaces, cards
Mist #ECE9E2 Fills, hover states
Ash #DBD7CE Hairlines, dividers
Stone #8C887F Tertiary text, captions
Graphite #46443E Secondary text
Ink #0D0D0D Wordmark, primary text
Signal #3E5C76 The only color in the system. Reserved for links, the primary button, and focus states, nothing else. If a screen needs more color than this to make sense, that's a content problem, not a palette problem.

03 · Typography

Three faces,
one job each.

Raleway carries the wordmark and nothing smaller, its warmth reads as considered at large sizes and slightly precious at body size. Inter takes over for everything that gets read: paragraphs, product UI, email. JetBrains Mono marks anything literal, a hex value, an env var, a price.
